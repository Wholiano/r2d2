🔧 How to build R2 — the app's missing instructions

The step-by-step build used to live inside the app, so the box alone doesn't show it. Here it is, pieced together from littleBits' own lesson and a builder's walkthrough. For this controller you only need Power + Control Hub + Servo + DC Motor — the Proximity Sensor is optional (its self-driving was an app feature that's gone now).

The 6 Bits

  • P4 Power (dark blue) + 9V battery & adapter
  • Control Hub — the brain & Bluetooth; ports 1 (top), 2 (middle), 3 (bottom)
  • 011 Servo — steering
  • 025 DC Motor — drive
  • Proximity Sensor (pink) + Wire — optional

Wire up the Bits

They snap together magnetically, left → right:

  1. 9V battery → adapter → plug into P4 Power.
  2. P4 Power → Control Hub, on the hub's single-adapter (input) side.
  3. Into the Control Hub's ports:
    • Port 2 (middle) → 011 Servo
    • Port 3 (bottom) → 025 DC Motor
    • Port 1 (top) → Wire → Proximity Sensor (optional)

Build the body

  1. Fit the gear (big side / small side) into the chassis, lock it with the axle, then push a wheel onto each end.
  2. Mount the DC motor under the chassis so its gear meshes with the chassis gears — that's what spins the wheels.
  3. Mount the servo at the front (the section with four holes). The small wheel goes on the servo's peg, the cover clips on top — this steers R2.
  4. Clip the battery holder + battery at the back; keep the hub and its lights toward the front.
  5. Snap on the frame (locking tab at the bottom — don't pinch the cables), add the arms (L→L, R→R, leave room for the wheels to spin), then the head. Stickers last.
If something's off: nothing drives or steers, or the wrong part moves → swap the Servo and Motor between ports 2 and 3. Forward/back or left/right reversed → just flip the Invert switches up in Settings, no rewiring needed.
