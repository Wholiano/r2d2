🔧 How to build R2 — the app's missing instructions

The step-by-step build only ever existed inside the app, which is why the box stops short. These steps are recovered from the original app's own mission screens, so they're the real sequence — not a guess.

The 6 Bits

  • P4 Power (dark blue) + 9V battery & adapter
  • Control Hub — the brain & Bluetooth; ports 1 (top), 2 (middle), 3 (bottom)
  • 011 Servo — steering
  • 025 DC Motor — drive
  • Proximity Sensor (pink) + Wire — optional

Part 1 — Build the circuit

  1. Battery → adapter → plug into the Power Bit, then snap Power → Control Hub on the hub's single-adapter (input) side.
  2. Snap the DC Motor Bit to BitSnap 3 on the Control Hub.
  3. Snap the Servo Bit to the CENTER BitSnap.
  4. Set the Servo's little switch to turn — not swing. In swing mode it waves instead of steering. (The DC Motor has its own direction switch next to it.)
  5. Add the wheel hook + small wheel to the Servo Bit.
  6. Power on. The light on the Power Bit should glow red.
  7. Optional: Wire → Proximity Sensor into BitSnap 1. Its self-driving was an app feature and is gone — R2 drives fine without it.

Part 2 — Build the drive

  1. Set your circuit aside.
  2. Click the mounting block onto the wheel frame, one side at a time.
  3. Attach the drive gear and axle to the wheel frame.
  4. Press the large wheels onto the axle — hard, until they're properly tight.
  5. Press your circuit firmly onto the FRONT of the mounting block.
  6. Press the battery firmly onto the BACK. Test it — it should stay put when you turn the whole thing upside down.
  7. Add the spike gear onto the DC Motor Bit.
  8. Click the DC motor assembly into the clips UNDERNEATH the wheel frame.
  9. Press the third-leg assembly onto the wheel frame.
  10. Power on (light red), then connect up top and drive.
⚠ This is the step that makes or breaks it. When you clip the motor under the wheel frame, the spike gear on the motor must physically touch the drive gear on the axle. If those two gears aren't meshed, the motor spins happily and the wheels don't move at all. The app asked this exact question: "Is the spike gear touching the drive gear?"
Other fixes: no steering at all → the Servo is almost certainly in swing mode instead of turn. Nothing responds, or the wrong part moves → check the Motor is on BitSnap 3 and the Servo on the center BitSnap. Forward/back or left/right reversed → flip the Invert switches up in Settings, no rewiring needed.
