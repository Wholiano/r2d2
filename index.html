🔧 How to build R2 — step by step, with pictures

The step-by-step build only ever existed inside the app, which is why the box stops short. These steps are recovered from the original app's own mission screens, so they're the real sequence — not a guess.

First — find these pieces

Every step below uses these names. Match them to the plastic pieces in your box before you start.

Wheel frameThe flat tray covered in little holes. Everything attaches to this.
Mounting blockThe tall upright board. Bits clip on the front, battery on the back.
AxleThe long thin rod. Runs through the tray; wheels push on its ends.
Drive gearGoes on the axle, in the tray. The build video calls it the smaller gear.
Spike gearThe spiky one. Pushes onto the DC motor.
Large wheels ×2The two big wheels. They push onto the axle ends.
Third legSmall wheel on a stalk. Clips under the front.

Your 5 Bits (the electronics)

  • Power Bit (dark blue) + battery & adapter
  • Control Hub — the brain and Bluetooth. Its three sockets are called BitSnaps.
  • Servo — does the steering
  • DC Motor — drives the wheels
  • Proximity Sensor (pink) + Wire — optional, not needed to drive

Part 1 — the circuit

1
Plug the battery into the Power Bit, then snap the Power Bit onto the Control Hub (the hub's input side — the end with a single connector).
2
Snap the DC Motor into BitSnap 3 and the Servo into the CENTER BitSnap. This matters — the hub expects each one in its own socket.
3
On the Servo there's a tiny switch marked swing and turn. Slide it to turn. On swing it just waves instead of steering.
4
Put the wheel hook and small wheel onto the Servo. Then switch the power on — the light on the Power Bit should glow red.

Part 2 — the drive

5
Set the circuit aside. Click the mounting block (tall board) down onto the wheel frame (flat tray), one side at a time.
6
Slide the axle through the bottom of the wheel frame with the drive gear on it, sitting in the middle.
7
Push a large wheel onto each end of the axle. Press hard — they need to be properly seated or they'll slip off later.
8
Press your Bits onto the FRONT of the tall board, and the battery onto the BACK. Turn it upside down — the battery should stay put.
9
Push the spike gear onto the DC Motor's shaft.
10
Clip the motor into the clips UNDERNEATH the wheel frame — and make sure the spike gear is actually touching the drive gear. See the diagram just below; this is the step that decides whether R2 moves.
11
Clip the third leg on underneath the front, then power on (red light). Open this page in Bluefy, hit Connect, and drive.
⚠ This is the step that makes or breaks it. When you clip the motor under the wheel frame, the spike gear on the motor must physically touch the drive gear on the axle. If those two gears aren't meshed, the motor spins happily and the wheels don't move at all. The app asked this exact question: "Is the spike gear touching the drive gear?"
Other fixes: no steering at all → the Servo is almost certainly in swing mode instead of turn. Nothing responds, or the wrong part moves → check the Motor is on BitSnap 3 and the Servo on the center BitSnap. Forward/back or left/right reversed → flip the Invert switches up in Settings, no rewiring needed.
